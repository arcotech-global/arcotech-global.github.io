AI-driven safety assurance

Integrated safety for oil and gas plants

With the Oil & Gas AI Brain at its center, ground robots, fixed monitoring points and the drone fleet form a plant-wide safety sensing network that covers every asset.

  • Large-leak detection: automatic detection and alarm of a 1 m × 1 m gas plume at 100 m range.
  • Mobile small-leak survey: H₂S, combustible gas (LEL), H₂ and CO; H₂S detection limit <1 ppm, response time <10 s.
  • Gauge reading: high-definition visible-light imaging of field gauge faces, read automatically by image-recognition algorithms.
Specifications

Abnormal-noise analysis: a high-sensitivity microphone on the pan-tilt head captures directional equipment audio.

Abnormal-temperature analysis: infrared thermography with real-time alarms on threshold exceedance.

Incipient fire detection

A flame-feature library of 100 million-plus samples and more than 30 recognition models detect incipient flames within 3–5 seconds at tank farms, truck loading racks, hot-oil pumps and high-temperature piping. Pop-up, audible and SMS alarms are raised simultaneously and pushed to the fire brigade and emergency-response systems.

Control of work

End-to-end contractor management (face recognition, RFID, IoT closed loop); AI detection of safety violations (coveralls, hard hats, gas-cylinder separation distance at hot work); electronic permit to work signed on site with high-precision 5G/BeiDou positioning; precise personnel location and historical track playback on the 3D plant model.

Drone platform

An integrated hardware and software system for unmanned inspection in the high-temperature, high-dust, high-EMI environments of oil and gas plants and production well sites.

  • Industrial-grade multirotor platforms and a long-endurance fixed-wing aircraft (HC-526), covering near and far field at low and high altitude.
  • Thermal imaging, high-zoom visible light, remote laser methane detection and LiDAR, plus loudspeaker, emergency water-sampling and rope-rescue payloads.
  • Two dock configurations for 24/7 automated takeoff and landing, data backhaul and automatic charging or battery swap at remote stations and border-region pipelines; dock scheduling and AI analytics are Arcotech's own.
